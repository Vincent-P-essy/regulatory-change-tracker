# EU 2022/2554 — 2023-01-16 to 2026-06-30

Digital operational resilience for the financial sector (DORA)

**10 changes create, withdraw or alter an obligation.** 1 substantive, 1 editorial. 31 provisions are untouched.

## No control claims these

Either the register has a gap here or the entity is out of scope. Someone has to decide which.

- **Article 19(5)** (added) — new provision containing 2 binding term(s): shall
  > Where a major ICT-related incident affects clients, financial entities shall inform those clients without undue delay and shall report to the competent authority on the measures taken to mitigate the effects.
- **Article 25(3)** (added) — new provision containing 2 binding term(s): shall
  > Financial entities shall maintain evidence of the remediation of every vulnerability rated critical or high, and shall record the date on which remediation was completed.
- **Article 28(4)** (added) — new provision containing 2 binding term(s): shall
  > Financial entities shall identify, for each contractual arrangement, whether it supports a critical or important function and shall record that determination in the register of information.
- **Article 6(5)** (added) — new provision containing 2 binding term(s): shall
  > Financial entities shall ensure that the control function referred to in paragraph 4 reports directly to the management body and shall document any instance in which its advice was not followed.
